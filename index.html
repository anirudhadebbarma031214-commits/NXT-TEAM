01 / Who we are

Not the next.
The NXT.

NXT TEAM is a creative technology collective focused on experimenting, learning and shipping projects that feel different. A new identity, the same drive to create.

THE ORIGIN / BEFORE NXT

One chapter ended.
The next began.

Our journey began with ANI STUDIO — a place for games, websites, music and technology. Then came an unexpected stop: ANI STUDIO was banned. It felt like the end of the road, but the dream was bigger than one name.

CHAPTER 01

ANI STUDIO

The beginning of the creative journey. A name built around curiosity, experiments and big ideas.

CHAPTER 02

The setback

When ANI STUDIO was banned, the original chapter had to stop. But the passion to create stayed alive.

CHAPTER 03

The comeback

Instead of giving up, we took everything we learned and started again with a new identity.

CHAPTER 04 / THE NEXT ERA

NXT STUDIO.
