The NXT.

NXT TEAM is a creative technology collective focused on experimenting, learning and shipping projects that feel different. A new identity, the same drive to create.

THE ORIGIN / BEFORE NXT

One chapter ended.
The next began.

Our journey began with ANI STUDIO — a place for games, websites, music and technology. Then came an unexpected stop: ANI STUDIO was banned. It felt like the end of the road, but the dream was bigger than one name.
